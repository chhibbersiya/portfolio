The reporting process

During a paid four-month journalism internship, I pitched stories in weekly editorial meetings, worked with an advisor, verified sources, and revised through editorial review. My brief was to make the opioid crisis understandable to other young people.

Changing habits to break addiction

Published April 13, 2026. I used behavioral research to explore how environment and cues shape habit formation. The writing challenge was to explain the mechanism through a story a reader could follow.

Belief systems and opioid addiction

Published April 21, 2026. This piece examined personal conviction and medical decisions through the cases of Steve Jobs and Prince. Editorial feedback led me to rebuild the framing around independently sourced events and separate evidence from blame.

The depiction of addiction in cinema

Published July 12, 2026. I interviewed film-studies instructor Antonio Alfaro in an eight-question Q&A about portrayals of substance use, realism, narrative choices, and social responsibility.

Beyond ‘Just Say No’

Published July 26, 2026. I developed a policy argument about evidence-based prevention, drawing on research and government reporting. Each criticism needed to rest on evidence readers could examine.
