Science at a child’s scale

Working with young visitors at Chabot made me think about how a scientific idea becomes a story a child wants to follow. I wrote and illustrated A Day of Discovery at Chabot and Sally’s Rockets as another way to invite that curiosity.

A Day of Discovery at Chabot

The book’s first 25-copy run sold out at Chabot in three months. I also completed four personalized commissions, adapting the work for individual readers.

Sally’s Rockets

For the next book, the work extended beyond the pages. I developed a retail pitch deck, consignment proposals, and an ROI calculator, then contacted eight retailers. One purchased copies after seven did not respond.

The project required decisions about illustration and storytelling alongside practical questions about printing cost, retail price, and how to make a pitch useful to a shop.

Following the whole journey

This work gave me experience with both making a creative product and getting it into someone else’s hands. The reader, the buyer, and the retailer all needed something different from the same book.
