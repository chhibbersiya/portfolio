Research in drone safety & urban air mobility

Drone safety in emergency response

My 2025 research contributed to Applying Specific Operations Risk Assessment (SORA) to UAS Emergency Response Missions. I am a co-author of the project poster archived on NASA’s Technical Reports Server.

The ground matters to air taxis

For the air-taxi corridor project, I owned the Ground Scenarios module. This involved combining household travel, Census, ACS, and OpenStreetMap data to build synthetic populations and ground networks for a transportation simulation.

The work connected a proposed air-mobility system to the trips people make on the ground. Preparing those inputs was part of making the simulation meaningful.

Question the input, too

While debugging a simulation problem, I traced the issue back to the data pipeline I had built. The experience reinforced a habit I also use in my hardware projects: check the assumptions behind a result, including my own.
